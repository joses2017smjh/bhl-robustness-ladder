## H3 — does a short IMU history help under unseen delay?

The comparison isolates information rather than network size. Both actors
have **63 inputs**: the original 45 observations plus 18 appended IMU values.
The history actor receives the current delivered gyro/gravity packet and
three previously delivered packets. The feedforward control repeats the
current delivered packet in the extra slots. Both have the same architecture,
initial weights, input dimensions and clean 48-input critic. The history
contains received measurements, never future samples or undelayed truth.

Both arms fine-tune the same frozen `dr-default-s0/model_5999.pt`. The original
45 actor columns, critic and action-distribution parameters are retained;
the extra actor columns start at zero, making initial predictions identical.
Optimizers are fresh. Each arm uses training seeds **0, 1, 2**, 4,096 parallel
environments and **500 additional PPO iterations**. Only final iteration
**499** is evaluated. These are six adaptations from one declared parent,
not six independent from-scratch training runs.

The parent's simulation interval is 0.005 s with decimation eight:
**25 Hz policy ticks, 40 ms per tick**. Training samples IMU delivery delay
of zero or one tick per episode. Evaluation uses fixed delays of
**0, 40, 80 and 120 ms**; 80 ms is the primary held-out condition and 120 ms
is a descriptive stress condition. These are simulated observation delays,
not command delays or measured hardware latencies.

The frozen evaluation grid is six commands × five new reset seeds
(201000–201004) × four delay conditions × two arms × three training seeds:
**720 episodes**. Each episode lasts ten seconds, with a one-second settling
period. Physics runs at 0.002 s; policy/packet updates remain at 0.04 s.
All conditions, timeouts, falls and tracking failures are retained.

An episode qualifies only if it survives the entire episode and achieves
at least half the commanded progress after settling, separately for every
active translation/yaw component. Translation uses realized body velocity
projected onto the commanded velocity; yaw uses realized signed yaw rate.
Standing still cannot qualify for a moving command. Falls, survival,
qualified counts, tracking errors and progress are reported separately.

**Scientific PASS requires at least two of three matched training seeds to
meet all of these clauses:**

- At 80 ms, history has at least **six more qualified episodes out of 30**
  than its matched feedforward control.
- At zero delay, history survives at least **27/30** episodes.
- At zero delay, history loses at most **one qualified episode** relative
  to its matched control.

Missing, duplicate, mislabeled or invalid records make the report INCOMPLETE;
a complete result failing the rule is NEGATIVE. These are newly declared H3
engineering criteria, not achieved results or a hardware safety guarantee.
Episode resets are shared across commands/arms and must be treated as blocks
for statistical analysis, rather than 720 independent environments.

[H3 protocol](../results/h34-campaign-20261008/h3/protocol.json) contains the
commands, teacher hashes, cells and exact gate values. History-based
adaptation is motivated by [RMA, RSS 2021](https://arxiv.org/abs/2107.04034)
and [A-RMA](https://arxiv.org/abs/2205.15299). This four-packet ablation is
a smaller question; it does not reproduce either system or inherit its results.
The [Berkeley humanoid locomotion work](https://learning-humanoid-locomotion.github.io/)
also motivates adaptation from action/observation history. Its hardware results
remain separate from this simulated IMU-delay ablation.

## H4 — does command-latched heading help the 22-DoF recipe?

R1, R2 and R1H retain their completed negative results. R1H seed 2 passes its
straight-walk checks but reaches only 8/10 qualification turns. R1H's
heading-hold reward uses accumulated heading relative to a resample reference;
its feedforward actor does not explicitly observe accumulated heading. This
motivates a new observation experiment, not a retry with relaxed thresholds.

The new **R1HO** arm fine-tunes the fixed R1H seed-2 final checkpoint with
independent fine-tuning seeds **0, 1, 2**, for **1,000 added PPO iterations**
each. Final iteration **999** is fixed. The robot stays at **22 actuated
joints**; the existing R1H physics, domain randomization, rewards and gait
clock remain. This is separate from the modified 24-DoF Waiter robot.

Two features are appended to actor 77 → **79** and critic 80 → **82**:

```text
error = yaw - reference_yaw
features = [sin(error), cos(error) - 1]
features = zero unless abs(command_wz) < 0.05 rad/s
```

The reference latches at reset and any change in the observable float32
command vector, using exact comparison (tolerance zero). Training and
deployment use the same algebra and latch contract. Extra first-layer
columns start at zero; original weights and distribution parameters are
retained with a fresh optimizer.

The original reward still uses its hidden explicit-mode/resampling reference.
The added command-latched reference can differ on repeated standing draws;
it **does not make the entire original reward state observable**. The new
feature nevertheless supplies accumulated heading during a fixed zero-yaw
command. Evaluation reads simulator yaw, so a pass would remain an
oracle-heading simulation result until an estimator is separately validated.

**The original H4 rule is unchanged:** at least **2/3 seeds** must both pass
turn-test v2 and qualify. Qualification requires **at least 9/10 turns**,
absolute straight-walk drift **≤15° on at least 2/3 reset seeds**, and push
falls **≤9/60**. The original evaluator code and predicates are frozen.
No seed from another recipe can be pooled into this arm. H4 reuses its
existing benchmark cases; passing them is not a fresh-world or hardware
generalization claim.

[Singh et al., *Learning Bipedal Walking for Humanoids*](https://arxiv.org/html/2207.12644v2)
provide a related precedent: section IV-B supplies desired root heading in
the robot frame and a sine/cosine gait clock. Their controller actuates 12 leg
joints; this 22-DoF command-latched feature tests a different hypothesis and
does not reproduce their controller or performance.

[H4 protocol](../results/h34-campaign-20261008/h4/protocol.json) identifies
the parent, features, training seeds and original gate. All failed seed
results remain in the report. A negative complete campaign closes this
experiment while leaving the repeatable-turning objective open.

## Runtime checks and retained evidence

Use the existing Isaac Sim 5.1 / Isaac Lab 2.3.2 stack. Compatible GPU
partitions are selected explicitly; this PyTorch build cannot run training
kernels on the current V100 allocation. Both arrays are capped at one GPU
each, for **at most two concurrent campaign GPUs**. Smoke jobs use 64
environments and three iterations; scored training uses 4,096 environments.
The requested bounds are 45 minutes per smoke task and three hours per full
task; these are scheduler limits, not measured runtime forecasts.

Every full array depends on a **fresh passing smoke of the same frozen
archive**. Smokes check actual CUDA execution, observation/action dimensions,
initialization, training, export parity and a short non-scored MuJoCo rollout.
Smoke reset seed 100 is excluded from the scored cohorts.

[`h34_campaign.py`](../scripts/bench/h34_campaign.py) freezes source,
recursively pinned upstream assets, parent inputs and launch arguments. Jobs
verify all hashes, run in node-local scratch, and bind the shared training
tree read-only. A [container intake probe](../results/h34-campaign-20261008/runtime-intake.json)
checked the read-only bindings and isolated home/cache paths. Final checkpoints,
exported policies, configuration, logs and complete scored records are retained
in the durable home campaign directory; intermediate checkpoints stay in scratch.

H3 cell-level PASS means execution and its 120 records validate; the complete
six-cell paired report is NEGATIVE. H4's complete three-seed verdict is PASS.
Protocol, intake, submission and finalization receipts are retained without
replacing the original frozen inputs or excluding negative records.
